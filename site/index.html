Ask the way you would ask a person.

No timeline, no menus. These are real requests and what happens next.

Cut the dead air from this vlog.

Finds long pauses and filler words, removes them, and joins the rest so it still sounds natural.

Add captions to my lecture.

Transcribes every word with timing, tells speakers apart, and writes SRT or WebVTT, or burns them into the picture.

I can barely hear myself over the AC.

Reduces noise and room echo, evens out loud and quiet moments, and lowers the music while someone talks.

Make a thumbnail and upload it, unlisted.

Renders a readable cover and publishes to YouTube or Vimeo. Nothing leaves your computer until you ask.

Split the talk at every slide change.

Detects when slides change and pulls their text, so chapters line up with what was on screen.

Make a 15 second vertical short of my budgie.

Picks the liveliest moments, reframes them to 9:16, and exports a file ready to post.

Put the recital on a DVD for my parents.

Authors a DVD or Blu-ray with a menu and chapters, ready to burn.

Questions people ask first

Do I need editing experience?
No. Describe what you want and the agent plans and runs the steps. There is no editing software to learn.
Is this an AI video generator?
No. It does not invent footage. It finishes the video you actually shot: cuts, captions, sound, color and delivery.
Will my video be uploaded without me knowing?
No. Processing happens on your computer. Publishing to YouTube or Vimeo runs only when you ask, and each component publicly declares what it sends and where.
What happens to my original file?
Nothing. Results are written as new files, and every step is recorded so it can be redone or undone.
What computer do I need?
One that can run a bash command in a terminal. Some components need extra tools such as FFmpeg, and each one says what it needs.
What can it not do yet?
AI narration and dubbing, music and sound effect generation, and direct exchange with Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve are still being built. Progress is public on the live dashboard.
